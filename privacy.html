Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who this covers

StickPrompt Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the StickPrompt YouTube channel (@stickprompt). It is not offered to the public. No other person signs in to it.

What Google data the tool uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for permission to manage the StickPrompt YouTube channel. It uses that permission only to:

  • upload lesson videos to the StickPrompt channel,
  • set video titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times,
  • add videos to the channel's playlists, and
  • read the channel's own video and playlist details to confirm an upload worked.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data from viewers or from any other YouTube account.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for ads or to train AI models.

How sign-in is stored

The sign-in token is held by the upload service the channel owner connects (Composio) so uploads can run on a schedule. It is used only for the actions listed above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

StickPrompt Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party access. Once removed, the tool can no longer reach the channel.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/srtech79/stickprompt/issues.

StickPrompt is an independent channel and is not affiliated with Anthropic.
