StickPrompt Uploader

StickPrompt is a YouTube channel that teaches Claude Code from beginner to expert, one simple lesson every week.

StickPrompt Uploader is a small private tool used only by the owner of the StickPrompt YouTube channel. It signs in to that one channel with Google and does three things:

  • Uploads new lesson videos to the StickPrompt channel.
  • Sets each video's title, description, thumbnail and publish time.
  • Adds the video to the course playlist.

It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.
